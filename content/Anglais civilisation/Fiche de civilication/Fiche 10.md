# **Synthesis – Key Events and Dates in U.S. Immigration History**

### **1. Early French and English Colonization**

- In **1673**, the French claimed the Mississippi River territory and called it **Louisiana**.
    
- In **1718**, they founded **New Orleans**.
    
- By the mid-18th century, New France had only **80,000 inhabitants**, while the English colonies had **1.5 million settlers**.
    

---

### **2. 19th-Century European Immigration**

**English immigration**

- Economic hardship and new machinery pushed many English workers to emigrate.
    
- In **1830**, 15,000 people left Liverpool, and around **1842**, 200,000 emigrated.
    

**Irish immigration**

- Many Irish arrived after **1816**, working on canals and railroads.
    
- The **1845 potato famine** caused massive starvation: 1 million died and 2 million emigrated by **1854**.
    

**German immigration**

- The **1848 revolutions** triggered a large wave of German migration.
    
- By **1858**, 1 million Germans had arrived.
    
- Between **1820 and 1920**, about **5.5 million** Germans migrated to the U.S., the largest European group.
    

**French and Italian immigration**

- The failed **1848 French revolution** and the **1870 war** brought refugees.
    
- Massive Italian immigration occurred after **1870**, with
    
    - 655,000 arrivals from **1890 to 1900**,
        
    - and 2 million more from **1900 to 1910**.
        

**Central and Eastern Europeans**

- From **1820 to 1920**, millions came from the Austro-Hungarian Empire, Russia, and Scandinavia.
    

---

### **3. Asian Immigration**

**Chinese immigrants**

- The first Chinese arrived in **1848**, working in mining and railroad construction.
    
- Numbers grew from **2,000 in 1850** to **107,000 in 1880**.
    
- In **1882**, the U.S. passed harsh laws limiting Chinese immigration.
    

**Japanese immigrants**

- Japanese communities formed in Hawaii and California, creating “Japantowns.”
    

---

### **4. Immigration Controls**

- **Ellis Island** operated from **1892 to 1954**, processing over **12 million** immigrants.
    
- **Angel Island** handled Asian immigrants between **1910 and 1940**.
    
- In **1907**, Ellis Island received 1.25 million newcomers.
    

---

### **5. Hispanic Immigration**

- After **1959**, Cuban refugees arrived following Castro’s rise to power.
    
- In the **1980s**, many “Marielitos” fled Cuba.
    
- Millions of Mexicans crossed the border, often without documents.
    
- By the **2020s**, Hispanic Americans numbered about **53–62 million**, around **18–19%** of the population.
    

---

### **6. Major Immigration Policies**

- In **1790**, the U.S. counted 4 million people.
    
- By **1900**, the population had reached 76 million.
    
- From **1900 to 1914**, more than 1 million immigrants arrived each year.
    
- From **1924 to 1965**, immigration was restricted to **150,000 a year**.
    
- In **1965**, President Johnson raised the immigration cap to **1 million per year**.
    
- By **2050**, Hispanics are expected to make up **29%** of the U.S. population.
